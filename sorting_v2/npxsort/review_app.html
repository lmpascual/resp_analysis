Red dots: this unit's spikes at their aligned extremum on the peak channel (pale red ticks in the strip = spike the matched unit didn't find). Amber triangles: spikes inside the ISI threshold. Purple diamonds: footprint mismatch, i.e. the spike's amplitude pattern across the unit's top channels differs from the template's by more than 50% after scaling, and by more than 6 noise SDs. Dark rings: the same event is also assigned to a nearby unit (largest channels within 100 µm) within 0.1 ms, counted only for unit pairs that coincide at 0.1 ms at least 4x more often than at 0.5-2.5 ms. Green strip: best-matching unit from the other sorter (pale = not shared). Coloured dots/rows: nearby units of the same sorter. Traces are the preprocessed (CatGT 300–9000 Hz + local CAR) voltage both sorters read.
